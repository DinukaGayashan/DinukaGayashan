01 About Me

A Human Behind the Code

I'm Dinuka Gayashan, a passionate Software Engineer currently working at LSEG (London Stock Exchange Group). With a strong foundation in Computer Engineering from the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, I bring both technical depth and creative curiosity to every project I build.

My journey in technology began with a fascination for how things work and has evolved into a career dedicated to building meaningful digital solutions. From backend architectures to cross-platform applications, I thrive at the intersection of complex problems and elegant code.

Beyond the screen, I'm an avid hobbyist photographer who believes that observation and attention to detail are skills that translate across every discipline. Whether I'm writing code or composing a shot, I aim for clarity, intention, and craft.

I graduated with Second Class Honours (Upper Division) and two Dean's List awards. My internship at Axiata Digital Labs shaped my approach to production-grade software and agile workflows.
